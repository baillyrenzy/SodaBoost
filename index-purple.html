Daily Wellness

A supplement works best as one part of a bigger picture. Here are the habits that give SodaBoost™ the best foundation.

Energy: build it from the ground up

Steady energy comes mostly from daily choices. A gummy supplement can sit on top of these basics.

Sleep

Aim for a regular sleep schedule. Consistent bed and wake times help you feel more alert during the day.

Hydration

Even mild dehydration can make you feel tired. Keep water within reach and sip through the day.

Balanced meals

Combine protein, fibre and healthy fats to avoid big swings between meals.

Metabolic support: small habits, steady effort

"Metabolic support" describes a lifestyle approach, not a magic switch. These everyday actions matter most.

  • Walk after meals, even for 10 minutes
  • Choose whole foods more often than ultra-processed snacks
  • Strength train two to three times a week if your doctor agrees
  • Limit sugary drinks and keep portions sensible
  • Get regular check-ups and discuss supplements with your doctor
